DeepSeek Widget - Quick AI Chat, Voice and Camera from Your Home Screen

One-tap access to DeepSeek AI from your Android home screen. Type, speak, or snap a photo and send it straight to DeepSeek. No unlocking required.

Features:
- Instant Chat: One tap opens DeepSeek directly to chat
- Voice-to-Chat: Tap mic, speak, and the text goes to DeepSeek
- Camera-to-Chat: Take a photo and send it to DeepSeek for analysis
- Text Input: Type a question directly from the widget
- Quick Prompts: Pre-built shortcuts for Summarize, Translate, Explain Code, Write Email
- Light/Dark Theme: Follows your system theme
- Resizable Widget: Works at 2x2, 4x1, 2x4, and other sizes
- Lightweight: Around 1.5 MB, no background services, no battery drain
- Privacy First: No data collected. Just a router to the official DeepSeek app.
- Open Source: MIT licensed, fully auditable code.
